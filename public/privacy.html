🦷 MouthLife: The Plaque Dimension — Privacy & Educational Notice

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applies to Web & iOS builds

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

MouthLife: The Plaque Dimension does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, analytics, device identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry. There are no user accounts, no tracking cookies, and no third-party advertising SDKs.

2. 100% On-Device Storage & Portable Sync Codes

  • All gameplay progress, Habit Stamps, audio preferences, and optional display profile settings are stored locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage (mouthlife_save_v2 and mouthlife.audioPrefs).
  • When you export an ML2: Cross-Device Sync Code or a .mouthlife.json backup file, that code is generated entirely on your device so you can copy and paste it yourself between your own devices.
  • You can reset and erase all local save data at any time inside the in-app ☁️ Sync menu.

3. Educational & Entertainment Disclaimer (Not Medical Advice)

MouthLife: The Plaque Dimension is an educational game and hands-free 2-minute brushing pacer designed to make real-world oral biology concepts (such as the Stephan pH curve, Modified Bass 45° sulcus brushing, C-shape interdental flossing, fluorapatite remineralization, and Universal/FDI tooth numbering) engaging and approachable.

The content in this app is for informational and educational entertainment only and is not a substitute for professional dental advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a licensed dentist or dental hygienist with any questions regarding your oral health.

4. Optional External Charity Links

The app includes optional outbound links to Smile On U, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting charitable dental care. Opening those external links is entirely optional and governed by the destination website's own policies.
