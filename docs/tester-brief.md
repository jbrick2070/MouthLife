# MouthLife: tester brief (web app, real devices)

Thank you for testing MouthLife. Please read this first; it takes five minutes.

## What it is

MouthLife is a 3D game and a daily 2-minute tooth-brushing timer for children aged 5 to 10. It is
free, has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and keeps everything on the device. You play it in
a browser, and it can be installed to the Home Screen like an app.

**Link:** https://jbrick2070.github.io/MouthLife/

## Devices (please tell us exactly which you used)

1. **An Android phone, Chrome:** required. This is also our preview of the coming Android app.
2. **An iPhone, Safari:** required.
3. **A Windows or Mac computer, Chrome, with keyboard and mouse:** required.
4. Bonus if you have one: a foldable Android phone (open flat and half open), an Android tablet or
   iPad, or a touchscreen laptop.

## If you have 1 hour

Do these, in order, and skip the rest:

1. **Android phone, Chrome, fresh browser (15 min):** the Prologue, then Home, then one whole
   2-Minute Brush (with floss; about 3 minutes). Note anything confusing or broken.
2. **Android phone (15 min):** one city stage (Dive): thumbstick, Floss, Scan, pause, and finishing
   the city if you can.
3. **iPhone, Safari (15 min):** the same 2-Minute Brush and a few minutes of a city stage.
4. **Computer (10 min):** a city stage with mouse and keyboard, including right-click to floss.
5. **Write-up (5 min):** your top bugs (with a screenshot or recording each) and first impressions.

## What to test (about 2 to 3 hours in all)

Start with a fresh browser (a private window, or clear the site's data) so you see the first-run
experience.

1. **The Prologue** (the first screens): read the story cards; tap the hardness chips; drag to turn
   the crystal; try "Have a Sync Code?" (you can paste a code you make in step 7). Then "Enter
   Arch".
2. **Home:** rub the teeth (they play notes), drag the sky to turn it, pick a city, try Treat, open
   How to Play. Check the Dive button names the city ("Dive 1 of 5 · Toronto" or the city alone on
   a small phone).
3. **The 2-Minute Brush:** do the whole thing at least once on a phone: 2 minutes of brushing, then
   floss, then rinse if it is on. Listen to the voice and music; pause and resume; switch to
   another app in the middle and come back; check the screen does not go to sleep.
4. **A city stage (Dive):**
   - Steer with the thumbstick and by touching the teeth; lift your thumb mid-drive (it should
     stop).
   - Drive into the pink gum walls (you should not get through).
   - Use Floss (tap, and drag the ring to aim), Zip through a narrow gap, Scan and Surge.
   - Pause with the house or pause button, and by switching apps.
   - Finish a city: the finale should play, then the score.
5. **On the computer:** steer with the mouse and keyboard; right-click to floss; check the hints
   say "click" (not "touch").
6. **Languages:** in Settings switch to Spanish, French, German and Japanese; look for words that
   are cut off or run over buttons.
7. **Sync Code:** in Settings, copy your Sync Code on one device and restore it on another (or in
   the Prologue on a fresh browser); your progress should come across.
8. **Install and offline:** "Add to Home Screen" on Android and iPhone, open it from the icon, then
   turn on airplane mode and play again.
9. **Feel:** is it smooth? Does the phone get hot? Is anything confusing or frustrating? Would a
   5-year-old know what to do?

## What to send back

- **A bug list** (Google Sheet or similar), one row per issue: device and browser, steps to
  reproduce, what you expected, what happened, severity (blocker, major, minor, polish), and a
  screenshot or short screen recording.
- **A short summary:** what worked well, what did not, and your impressions as a player and as a
  parent would see it.

## Please do not

- Do not post reviews or ratings of MouthLife anywhere (store reviews tied to a paid test break the
  stores' rules).
- Do not include anyone's personal details in recordings or notes.
- You do not need to test the iPhone app, widgets or anything outside the link above; that comes
  in a later round.
