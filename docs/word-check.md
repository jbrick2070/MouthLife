# MouthLife word check (for the owner's review)

Made by `node scripts/i18n-readability.mjs` from the 1115 English strings the game can show.
It lists text on the play path that uses a lab word, a word of 12 letters or more, or more than
16 words in one sentence. Nothing has been changed: mark the rows to rewrite, and each
approved rewrite is then made once in the source and translated into the other 7 languages.

Flagged, by place: The stage: 24 · City names and zones: 2 · Home: 14 · 2-Min Brush: 4 · How to Play: 2 · The score: 3 · Upgrades: 3 · Science pages: 36 · Other screens: 10.

The science pages (the Prologue, the history almanac, the tooth chart: 36 flagged strings) are
science on purpose and are not listed. Suggested for them: one plain first line each.

The "plain word" column only shows words the game already uses elsewhere; an empty cell needs a
decision.
