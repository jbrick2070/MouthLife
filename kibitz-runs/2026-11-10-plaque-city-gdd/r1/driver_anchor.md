MUST-FIX BEFORE BUILD:
1. [§2.1, §3, §4.1] Mouthwash Surge win-condition soft-lock risk (CONFIRMED against docs/GDD_AND_ARCHITECTURE.md): §2.1 requires triggering a "final Mouthwash Remineralization Surge to seal the district", §3 gates Mouthwash on `Surge Meter == 100%`, and §4.1 ties Surge orbs to freeing finite Enamel Citizens. If a player spends Mouthwash mid-fight before reaching 90% cleanliness, they can run out of Citizen orbs and soft-lock district completion. Fix: Recharge Surge Meter continuously from active plaque scrubbing and biofilm slicing (with Citizen rescues granting burst bonus charge), AND allow sealing a district either via player-cast Mouthwash at >= 90% cleanliness or an automatic District Fluoride Seal cascade once >= 90% cleanliness + all district Citizens are rescued.
2. [§6.3.1 vs §2.1/§4.1] Contradictory cleanliness formula (CONFIRMED against docs/GDD_AND_ARCHITECTURE.md): §6.3.1 defines cleanliness first as continuous mean density `1 - sum(density)/count` and immediately parenthetically as binary cell count `density <= 0.10`. Fix: Standardize on ONE metric everywhere: binary clean-cell ratio (`count(paintable cells with density <= 0.10) / count(paintable cells)`), plus visual snap-to-sparkle when a cell crosses `<= 0.10` so the player can visually read what still needs scrubbing.
3. [§2.2 vs §1.1] Passive Brush-Along timer drifts toward generic habit-tracker feel (CONFIRMED against docs/GDD_AND_ARCHITECTURE.md): §1.1 promises "not another habit tracker" and experiential mechanics, yet §2.2 is mostly a passive 120s animation + a self-report "Floss Check!" button. Fix: Keep Brush-Along hands-free during actual brushing, but connect it tangibly to the game world: each completed 30s quadrant dispatches Captain Bristle's "Scout Crew" to pre-loosen stubborn fissure plaque / drop visible Supply Pods into Plaque City for the next Arcade run, and make the optional 20s Interdental Bonus an animated C-curve interdental pacer rather than an instant self-report button.
